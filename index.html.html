DAY 03

South Goa: Lagoons, Kayaks & Boats

☀️

Morning to Lunch

10:00 AM - 2:00 PM

Cola Beach OR Salaulim Dam

RELAX STATE

Spend your midday window swimming and chilling out by the calm, unique fresh emerald lagoon stream waters at Cola Beach. Alternatively, guide the vehicle inland to stand on the edge of the massive Salaulim Dam spillway architecture.

🌤️

Afternoon

Palolem & Butterfly Beach

🌅 SUNSET MISSION

Hit Palolem Beach for sunset kayaking along the quiet shoreline waters. If the crew decides to explore isolated **Butterfly Beach**, link up with local shore boat operators for a quick 10-minute speed shuttle run across the coastal rocks!

Part II

Gokarna & Inland Climb (Days 4-5)

DAY 04

The Coastal Run

☀️

Morning

Gokarna Exploration

TEMPLES & BEACHES

Spend your morning exploring the peaceful shores of Gokarna (Om Beach) or visiting the historic Mahabaleshwar Temple structure.

🌤️

Afternoon

Honnavar Mangroves

1 HOUR DRIVE

Check out and drive 1 hour south to Honnavar. Experience the famous elevated Mangrove boardwalk or take a quick, serene backwater boat ride.

🌅

Late Afternoon / Sunset

Murdeshwar Temple

OVERNIGHT STAY

Drive another 30 minutes south. Explore the massive Shiva statue and catch a stunning sunset right over the beach temple. Stay overnight in Murdeshwar or Gokarna.

DAY 05

The Inland Climb

🌲

Morning

Drive to Dandeli

4-5 HR DRIVE

Start early from the coast. The drive from Murdeshwar up to Dandeli takes about 4 to 5 hours (~190 km) as you climb away from the beaches into the dense forest roads.

🛶

Afternoon Onwards

River Adventure

WATER SPORTS

Arrive in Dandeli, check into your remote jungle stay, and head straight to the river for water rafting and adventure activities.
